# Appendix D｜AI–Human Responsibility Boundary

> 🌏 **中文版本：**  
> 请参见 [`docs/book/appendix/D-ai-human-responsibility-boundary.md`](../../book/appendix/D-ai-human-responsibility-boundary.md)

AI may assist production.
Humans remain responsible for completion.

Responsibility cannot be delegated to tools.

## Permitted AI Usage
- drafting
- editing
- translation
- structure suggestion

## Responsibility Rule
A human initiates execution,
the system verifies completion,
the human accepts the outcome.
